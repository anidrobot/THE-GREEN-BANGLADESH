# 🌿 Kocuri Pana: A Sustainable Green Business Model

## 📌 Overview
“Kocuri Pana” is an innovative green business idea that focuses on transforming water hyacinth (commonly known as Kocuri Pana) into eco-friendly and value-added products. This project aims to solve environmental problems while creating economic opportunities.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement
Water hyacinth is considered one of the most harmful aquatic plants in Bangladesh. It:
- Blocks waterways 🚫
- Reduces oxygen levels in water 🐟
- Harms aquatic life 🌊
- Creates environmental pollution  

Despite being harmful, it grows rapidly and remains underutilized.

---

## 💡 Our Solution
We proposed a sustainable solution by converting water hyacinth into:
- 🌸 Natural Washroom Fragrance (from flowers)
- 🧺 Handmade eco-friendly products (future expansion)
- ♻️ Biodegradable materials

This approach helps:
- Reduce environmental pollution  
- Create low-cost raw materials  
- Promote green entrepreneurship
